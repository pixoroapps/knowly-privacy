This Privacy Policy describes how Pixoro Apps ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile application Knowly: Trivia Quiz (the "App"). By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Information collected automatically

When you use the App, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Device information: device model, manufacturer, operating system and version, system language, country/region, screen resolution, time zone.
  • Advertising identifier (AAID): your Google Advertising ID, used by advertising and analytics SDKs to deliver and measure ads. You can reset or opt out at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • App usage data: sessions, screens viewed, features used, buttons tapped, in-app events (e.g., questions answered, levels completed), session duration, in-app purchase events (non-financial metadata only).
  • Diagnostic data: crash logs, stack traces, performance metrics, error messages, and the state of the App at the moment of a crash.
  • Approximate location: coarse, IP-based country/region inferred by analytics and ad services (we do not collect GPS location).
  • Network information: IP address and connection type (used by third-party SDKs).

1.2. Anonymous account identifier

The App uses Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous account for each installation. This is a randomly generated identifier assigned by Firebase and is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any personal profile. It is used solely to sync your in-app progress (such as unlocked content, in-app purchase entitlements, and diamond balance) across sessions and, where applicable, between your devices.

1.3. In-app purchases

The App offers in-app purchases (such as diamonds and other virtual items). All payments are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment method, card number, or billing address. We only receive a non-financial purchase confirmation (transaction ID, product ID, purchase status) to grant you the purchased items.

1.4. Information we do NOT collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone input, camera input, precise (GPS) location, health data, or files on your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and operate the App and its core features;
  • Sync your progress and in-app purchases across sessions and devices;
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix bugs, crashes, and performance issues;
  • Analyze aggregated usage trends to improve gameplay, content, and user experience;
  • Serve, personalize, and measure advertising (through Google AdMob);
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our terms;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at pixoro.apps@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request". You can request deletion of data associated with your installation at any time by:

Once we verify a deletion request, we will delete data associated with that identifier within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain records (e.g., purchase receipts for tax compliance).
